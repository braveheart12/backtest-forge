Strategy

Analysis window

Crypto is non-stationary — early eras are a different, wilder asset. Fitting on the era that resembles the future beats fitting on all of history. Excluded early bars are kept for the era stress-test below.

Regime filters

Optional gates ANDed onto the position signal — when a gate is off-side the strategy stays (or goes) flat. Applied identically in backtests, sweeps and walk-forward.

Costs & capital

Risk & exits

Stops fill inside the bar (gaps fill at the open); after a protective exit, re-entry waits for a fresh signal. Sweeps and walk-forward use these settings too.

Honesty checks

Automated bias detectors for the CURRENT config. Lookahead: every signal must reproduce identically when the data ends at the signal's own bar — if it changes, the strategy peeked at the future. Warm-up: how much history must be fetched before live indicator values match the backtest. [DR-79]

Robustness — same config, every dataset

Runs the CURRENT config (strategy, filters, exits, sizing, fit-from date) on every stored dataset. A real edge agrees across exchanges and history depths; divergence is an era or venue tell. [DR-77]

Config library

Named configs — strategy, parameters, filters, exits, sizing — saved on this device. Export as JSON for backup or another device; freezing for a forward record stays on the Radar tab. [DR-77]

Cross-section lab — which family wins across the whole market?

Runs the fixed protocol grid for every MA family on the FULL Binance universe — selection-free at both layers (median-of-grid per asset, no best-cell picking) and judged by a correlation-robust panel-t (per-day cross-sectional mean excess vs each asset's own B&H, so BTC-beta cannot inflate significance). One-time fetch is cached for 7 days. [DR-86]
Floor $0 = the genuinely full universe. Note the grid warm-up still sets a history minimum per coin (TEMA needs 3× its slow period) — the short grid halves that at the cost of dropping the 160/200 slow columns, so results from the two grids are separate protocols, not directly comparable.
Survivorship patch — the graveyard [DR-87]
Delisted pairs vanish from the live API but their full history survives in Binance's public archive (data.binance.vision, monthly ZIPs — decompressed right here in the browser). Backfilling them makes the panel survivorship-corrected: B&H on dead coins usually ended −90%+, which is exactly where gates and exits earn their keep. One-time, resumable, WiFi recommended. Needs ≥ ~1,010 daily bars per coin to enter the panel (the grid's warm-up discipline).

Forward track record

Freeze a config here and the app builds its genuinely out-of-sample track record from that day forward — the only test that can never be overfit. Freeze from the Today card above; refresh data to extend the record. [DR-75]

Portfolio — allocator tournament

Multi-asset, long-only, periodic rebalancing with fees charged on turnover. Every allocator is rule-based — nothing is fitted, so overfitting risk is structurally low. All are judged against the 1/N null: an equal-weight BUY & HOLD of the same coins, which most optimizers fail to beat out-of-sample. [DR-80]
